Updated November 4, 2026. Source: the user-supplied six-page
`Research_Statement_Base.pdf`. This is a concise adaptation for a personal website;
the PDF itself is not bundled or offered for download.

- **Learning transferable biosignal representations:** statement pages 1–3.
  WildECG, BioCodec and BrainCodes; evaluation of generalization and physiological
  grounding; future comparison of learning objectives and hierarchical codes.
- **Understanding multimodal dynamics of behavior:** statement pages 3–4.
  PRECOG experimental contributions, point-process models of response timing,
  driving stressors, and future models of cross-system coupling.
- **Improving health outcomes and clinical translation:** statement pages 4–6.
  Eye tracking in mental health, pediatric visual assessment and daily-life
  sensing; reliability and personalized longitudinal assessment as future aims.

The prose uses first person and distinguishes completed studies from intended
directions. The proposed lab and lab-formation plans are omitted at the user's
request. Group differences are not described as validated clinical tools.
Experimental collaborations are described as contributions rather than sole
ownership. No generated diagrams or images were added.

Each page presents all research prose first, including future questions, followed
by one References section. Numbered inline links point to the full citations,
which include all authors, venue, year, direct links and expandable/copyable BibTeX. Existing
metadata comes from `publication-sources.json`. Explicitly excluded publications
remain excluded.
