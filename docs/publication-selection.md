- [PRECOG: Comprehensive Experimental Design and Stimuli Protocol for Investigating Depression and Suicidal Ideation](https://osf.io/preprints/osf/nxvz5_v1) — Experimental protocol and affective stimuli underpinning the PRECOG portfolio.
