Kleanthis Avramidis

Signal Analysis and Interpretation Lab ↗

I come from Athens, Greece. I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, working on Human-Centered Machine Intelligence under the supervision of Prof. Shrikanth Narayanan. I develop machine learning and biomedical signal processing methods that help augment human well-being, from transferable representations of physiological signals such as EEG, ECG and eye movements, to reliable measures of cognitive function and mental health.

Research

Learning transferable biosignal representations

How can we learn from heterogeneous signals and recording settings while preserving information that is relevant to common human function?

Read more →

Understanding multimodal dynamics of behavior

How do stimuli, context, and individual differences shape physiological and behavioral responses, and what do our derived measurements reflect?

Read more →

Improving health outcomes and clinical translation

Can these representations and models produce reliable measures of cognitive function, mental health, and temporal change that we could trace?
